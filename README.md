# Terminal-Dice-Roller
Addes alias of various D&amp;D dice so you can roll. supports d2 d4 d6 d8 d10 d12 d20 d100 and 1 roll or up to 10 of each by default. 
Run it once for temporary alias or add to .bashrc or .zshrc for it to persist. 


## example output
$ 1d6

4

$ 2d20

20 14 

$ 10d6           

6 2 4 1 6 3 2 4 6 4
